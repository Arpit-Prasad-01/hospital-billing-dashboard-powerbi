# Hospital Patient and Treatment Billing Dashboard

A 4-page Power BI report built on a cleaned dataset of 1,500 patients (Jan 2024 to Aug 2026).

## Pages
1. Executive Overview: billing, profit, margin, payment mix
2. Department & Region: revenue vs margin by department and region
3. Patient & Discount: patient value and discount impact
4. Outliers & Operations: outlier bills, stay length, admission type

## Key findings
- Total billing Rs 1.87 Cr, profit Rs 33.7 L, margin 18.07%
- Oncology has the highest billing but a margin of only 17.1%
- Discounts do not reduce margin (association, not cause)
- Satisfaction falls from 4.23 (1-2 day stays) to 3.61 (8+ days)
- Emergency admissions have the lowest satisfaction and margin
